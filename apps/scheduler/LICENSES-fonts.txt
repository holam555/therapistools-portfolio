Fonts used by the Scheduler
===========================

The Scheduler uses the shared Therapistools fonts, Fredoka and DM Sans.
They are self-hosted in shared/fonts so the app makes no third-party
requests, and both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
The full license texts are in shared/fonts/OFL-Fredoka.txt and
shared/fonts/OFL-DMSans.txt.
