Who the tools are for

Therapistools is a set of free browser tools for school-based therapists and K-12 educators: Worksheets, the Scheduler and the IEP Writer. By using them you agree to these terms. If you use them as part of your job, make sure that fits your school's or district's policies; those policies come first.

The tools are for adults. They are not meant to be used by children on their own.

Your work is yours

Everything you make with the tools, including worksheets, schedules, sentence banks, report templates and drafts, belongs to you. We claim no rights over it. Because almost all of it is saved in your own browser rather than with us, keeping a backup is also yours to do; each tool has an export button for that.

The IEP Writer can prepare a prompt for an AI chatbot. Whether you use one, which one, and what you paste into it is your choice, under that service's own terms. We recommend keeping student details out of it, the same as here.

Students and privacy

The tools are built so that a student's name never needs to be typed into them: students are placeholders or ID numbers, and the Worksheets name field is used for one printout and then forgotten. Please keep it that way. You are responsible for what you type, for following your district's rules, and for laws such as FERPA. We cannot see what you type, so we cannot check it for you.

The IEP Writer account

  • Signing in is optional. The account stores your report template and nothing else; the privacy page lists exactly what that means.
  • Sign in with Google or with a code sent to your email. Keep access to that email address; it is the only way into the account.
  • One account per person. We may close an account that is used to attack the service or to break these terms.
  • You can ask us to delete your account at any time through the feedback form, and we do it within a week. Signing out does not delete it.
  • The account service can be unavailable now and then. Your template also stays in your browser and in your backup file, and the tool keeps working without signing in.
